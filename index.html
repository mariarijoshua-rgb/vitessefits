About Vitesse Fits

Vitesse Fits is a fashion-focused clothing store bringing stylish, affordable and versatile clothing to customers who want to express themselves confidently.

From everyday basics to standout pieces, we stock clothing for men, women and anyone building a wardrobe that keeps up with their day. Our name comes from the French word for speed, because good style should never slow you down.
